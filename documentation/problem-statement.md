# VisionX – Intelligent Smart Walking Stick for Visually Impaired Individuals

Beyond Sight, Towards Independence.

Theme: Assistive Tech. Official challenge: Build mobility or touchless aid for specially abled users.

A traditional cane provides contact information at the tip. A shelf or projecting box can enter the user's path above that sweep. VisionX explores whether an inexpensive, forward-facing sensor pair can provide an audible cue before contact while preserving the cane's grip, tip and mechanical function. The engineering objective is a repeatable, offline demonstration of a one-metre warning envelope with familiar, distinguishable controls.
