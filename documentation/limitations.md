# Limits of the prototype

VisionX is an educational supplementary mobility aid. Keep established cane technique and the original load-bearing structure. A one-metre echo describes only a portion of the space in front of the current sensor angle. A user can move substantially between a warning and their response. Silence can also follow power loss.

Thin poles, fabric, irregular edges and oblique surfaces can return weak or displaced echoes. Below about 2 cm the transducer has a blind zone. Sunlight can swamp the IR detector, dark targets may absorb its light, and glossy targets may reflect it away or produce false alarms. A missing echo has several possible causes. The two sensors cannot establish that an entire route is clear.

Forward-facing sensors do not reliably detect steps, potholes or platform edges. Loud alarms can mask traffic or speech. Secure the bank, check cable strain relief, and set a comfortable sound level with a suitable buzzer. No waterproofing, drop testing, endurance testing, accessibility validation or mobility certification has been performed.

Demonstrate on a stationary stick with a sighted operator and a marked target track. Keep the path free of trip hazards. Before any user study, involve an orientation and mobility professional and get informed consent.
